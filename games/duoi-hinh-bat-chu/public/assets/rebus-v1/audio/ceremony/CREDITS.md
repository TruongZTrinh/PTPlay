# Award ceremony sounds

All recordings are CC0 and served locally with the game.

- `crowd-applause.ogg`: “Well Done” by qubodup, https://opengameart.org/content/well-done. The source changed to CC0 in 2024; its original filename predates that change.
- `firework-burst.ogg` and `firework-crackle.ogg`: `fw_01.ogg` and `fw_04.ogg` from “25 CC0 bang / firework SFX” by rubberduck, https://opengameart.org/content/25-cc0-bang-firework-sfx.
- `winner-fanfare.ogg`: “Happy and Sad Tuba Fanfare” by Trex0n (Cal McEachern), https://opengameart.org/content/happy-and-sad-tuba-fanfare.

CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
